About Me

I'm a Cybersecurity Engineering student at VIZJA University in Warsaw, Poland, passionate about building secure systems from the ground up. I'm drawn to the technical depth of network security — understanding not just how things work, but why they fail, and how to make them resilient.

I approach every lab and project with a detail-oriented mindset: I document everything, test thoroughly, and don't move on until I genuinely understand what's happening under the hood. Whether it's configuring VPN tunnels, capturing live traffic, or hardening a Linux system, I treat each task as an opportunity to build real, transferable skills.

I'm a fast learner who thrives on solving problems that don't have obvious answers. I enjoy breaking things in controlled environments, figuring out exactly what went wrong, and building something more secure as a result.
